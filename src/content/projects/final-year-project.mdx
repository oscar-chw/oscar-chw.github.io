## The problem

Small LLM agents that run locally are cheap and private, but they break the rules they are given. Prompting harder only goes so far.

## What I built

Final-year project (Prof. XU Hong): co-developed Bayesian scoring and conformal risk control for a 4B LLM, evaluated on a held-out synthetic tool-use testbed.

## Result

On that held-out synthetic testbed, tasks with rule violations fell by about 80% versus the prompted-agent baseline.

## How it works

The layer sits between the agent and its actions. It <Term t="conformal risk control">combines Bayesian scoring with conformal risk control</Term> to decide whether an action passes the configured decision rule.

## Limits

- The joint research is unpublished, so this page is an overview only, with no code link. The measured reduction is specific to the synthetic testbed; it is not a deployment benefit or general safety guarantee.
