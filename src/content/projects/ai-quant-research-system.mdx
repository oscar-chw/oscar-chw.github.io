## The centrepiece build

Its centrepiece is one complete build: over about four days in September 2026, a Claude Code orchestrator and its subagents turned López de Prado's *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* into 1,805 paraphrased, cited claims and a tested Python library. The result in [this repository](https://github.com/oscar-chw/agentic-quant-research): **1,400+ extracted claims verified by passing tests** (758 tests, 0 failing).

Across the whole project, the orchestrator and about 140 AI subagents (per private session logs) completed 266 gated tasks.
