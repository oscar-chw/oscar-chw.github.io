## The centrepiece build

Its centrepiece is a Python quant-research library built from López de Prado's *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*, using AI coding agents. The versioned vault links implementations to paraphrased, cited source claims and reproducible tests. The contribution is the reviewable connection from a claim to code and evidence, rather than an agent's assertion that it has finished.

The repository's dated build record lists 1,805 claims and 1,417 with all named tests passing at that revision. These are historical inventory counts, not a measure of research quality. The [evidence record](https://github.com/oscar-chw/agentic-quant-research/blob/f8c48256e12b96523807ba8f59c04b798517e04e/docs/evidence.md) explains coverage and limits.

<h2 id="crypto-desk-blueprint">Quant-system blueprint</h2>

<Part role="Designer; implemented with AI coding agents under my design and review" stack={["Python", "typed Protocols", "conformance tests"]} metrics={[{"value": "16 of 20", "label": "experiments, each with a control arm, support their rule; the other four are reported"}, {"value": "20", "label": "deliberately broken implementations, each caught by the conformance suite"}]} links={[{"label": "Repository: crypto-desk-blueprint", "href": "https://github.com/oscar-chw/crypto-desk-blueprint"}, {"label": "Demo: the blueprint and three of its experiments", "href": "/demos/crypto-desk/"}]} />

An asset-agnostic architecture for a quantitative research and trading system, for a person or an AI agent. The repository's current worked example uses crypto; it does not establish implementations for other asset classes. It splits the system into nine typed stages, from data and features through pricing, risk and portfolio sizing to execution and validation. Each stage has conformance tests that any implementation must pass, and tested building blocks: a point-in-time store that refuses future reads, a fee and slippage model, risk limits with a kill switch, purged cross-validation, deflated Sharpe and capped half-Kelly. No trading profit is claimed.
