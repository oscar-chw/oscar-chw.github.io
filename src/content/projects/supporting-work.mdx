## Crypto Desk Blueprint

<Part role="Designer; implemented with AI coding agents under my design and review" stack={["Python", "typed Protocols", "conformance tests"]} metrics={[{"value": "16 of 20", "label": "experiments, each with a control arm, support their rule; the other four are reported"}, {"value": "20", "label": "deliberately broken implementations, each caught by the conformance suite"}]} links={[{"label": "Repository: crypto-desk-blueprint", "href": "https://github.com/oscar-chw/crypto-desk-blueprint"}, {"label": "Demo: the blueprint and three of its experiments", "href": "/demos/crypto-desk/"}]} />

A template for building a crypto trading desk from scratch, for a person or an AI agent. It splits the desk into nine typed stages, from data and features through pricing, risk and portfolio sizing to execution and validation. Each stage has conformance tests that any implementation must pass, and tested building blocks: a point-in-time store that refuses future reads, a fee and slippage model, risk limits with a kill switch, purged cross-validation, deflated Sharpe and capped half-Kelly. No trading profit is claimed.
