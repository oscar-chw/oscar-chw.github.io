## The problem

IMC Prosperity is a multi-round algorithmic and manual trading competition. Each round adds new products, and each needs its own fair value, its own risk, and its own decision about when a signal is real. We were a team of 3: my teammates coded the early rounds and I coded the final two.

## What I built

- **<Term t="Kalman filter">Kalman/OU fair values for market making</Term>.** A Kalman filter replaced a fixed-window drift gate, so a drift signal is used only when its slope is clearly away from zero.
- **A gated one-lot relative-value trade** across a product family, capped at one lot and run beside market making on a subset of products.
- **The team's results check**, plus a round-by-round review against nine public write-ups from teams placed 2nd to 583rd.

## Result

Top 5% globally (904th of 18,803 teams) and 18th in Hong Kong.
