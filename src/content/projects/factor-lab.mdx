## The problem

Factor research fails quietly in three places: using prices before they were available, choosing a factor after peeking at the test window, and building portfolios that ignore turnover and limits. The Factor Lab rules out each of those mistakes by construction, not by discipline.

## What I built

- **Momentum vs reversal on lagged prices**, scored by <Term t="rank IC">Spearman rank IC</Term> on fixed train, validation and test dates.
- **Selection on validation only.** Test labels never enter selection, and only the chosen factor is scored, once, on the held-out test.
- **Portfolio construction as a convex optimization**: weights under risk and position limits with a turnover cost, checked against hand-solved cases.
- **Append-only, hashed trials.** Failed runs are kept, and any trial can be verified and recomputed later.

## Result

On synthetic data: the optimizer is checked against hand-solved cases, and every trial can be recomputed from its hashes.
